# Introduction cover artwork

These fixed 600 × 840 covers reproduce the approved P1–3 introduction study
(`intro-editorial-covers.html`). They are decorative examples, not store products
or user album documents. The home screen and P4 keep their existing artwork.

- `intro_travel.png`: `assets/frame_preview/ocean_shore.jpg`.
- `intro_family.png`: `parent_back.jpg` and `parent_hand.jpg` from
  `assets/templates/soft_babybook/images/approved_stock/`.
- `intro_daily.png`: `assets/frame_preview/spring_flowers.jpg`.
- Print lettering: existing `assets/fonts/Cormorant-Regular.ttf`; small captions
  use the study's sans serif fallback. Typography is baked into the artwork;
  no additional font is loaded by the introduction at runtime.

Exported directly from the approved native HTML cover composition at 3×, without
the surrounding app interface or shadow. Existing photograph source and license
information stays with the original asset directories.
